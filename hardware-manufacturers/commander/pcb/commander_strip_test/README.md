# Commander strip test board (KiCad 10), revision T0

Everything that is **new** on the Commander mainboard, without the codec core: the PoE converter, the
PoE / battery 5 V selector, the front battery port, the USB5744 hub with four SuperSpeed bays and the
USB2517 hub with five Hi-Speed bays. A stock **Radxa ZERO 3E** plays the codec core.

The point of this board is to prove the new circuitry on the bench while the RK3566 / LPDDR4 layout of
the mainboard is done properly. It is a 4-layer board with nothing finer than a QFN.

**Status: schematic complete and checked (306 parts, ERC clean); placed without a single collision; routed by the
mainboard's script on two signal layers: 725 of 984 connections (73.7 %), 5,758 track segments, 561 vias.
259 connections are still open. NOT ready for fabrication** — see "What is still open".

## How it relates to the mainboard

This project has no tools of its own. It is generated by the mainboard's tools from the same sources:

| Sheet | Source |
|---|---|
| `poe`, `battery` | the mainboard's own `30_poe.net` and `33_battery_port.net` (listed in `src/netlist/shared.txt`) |
| `usb3_hub`, `usb2_hub` | the same generator templates as the mainboard, minus the keyboard port |
| `testif` | `src/netlist/40_test_interface.net` — the only circuit that exists just here |

The strip has the mainboard's exact geometry (bays at 19.39 + n × 32.45 mm), so it fits the same rear
panel. The column is shortened to 72 mm and keeps the mainboard's PoE floorplan and isolation barrier.

Rebuild (from the mainboard folder): `python tools/build.py --variant strip_test`

## Connecting a ZERO 3E

| Connector | Goes to | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| **J50** (USB-C, strip front edge) | ZERO 3E USB 3 Type-C host port | USB 3 + USB 2 for the USB5744. This upstream port has one SuperSpeed lane wired and no mux (the bays do have one): if it enumerates at USB 2 only, turn the plug over |
| **J51** (USB-C, strip front edge) | ZERO 3E power / OTG Type-C port | 5 V **to** the ZERO 3E (2.5 A fuse), and USB 2 for the USB2517. The ZERO 3E must run this port as a host (`dr_mode = "host"`) |
| **J52** (1×4 header, rear edge) | the ZERO 3E's four PoE centre taps | PoE input, **up to 57 V — primary side** |
| **J53** (1×8 header, left edge) | ZERO 3E 40-pin header | GND, 3.3 V out, I2C3 SDA / SCL (FUSB302B), `TYPEC_INT`, `BATT_VBUS_EN`, `PWR_SRC_ST`, `BATT_VBUS_FLT_N` |
| **J54** (1×4 header, left edge) | optional | GND, hub reset, I2C2 (battery-module telemetry option) |
| **J40** (USB-C, front edge) | battery module / any 5 V USB-C source | as on the mainboard |

Without J53 wired the board still works: the hubs start on their own, and the battery port powers the
board from any USB-C source. The jumpers are only needed to charge a module or read the status lines.

## What differs electrically from the mainboard

- `VCC_SYS` comes from the 5 V bus through a ferrite (R915); `VCC_3V3` is the hub's own 3.3 V buck (R916).
- C601 / C602 are 0 Ω: the SuperSpeed AC coupling of the host transmitter already sits on the ZERO 3E.
- The USB2517 hangs on the ZERO 3E's OTG port instead of USB2 HOST2. Same hub, different root port.
- No keyboard port, microSD, eMMC, Ethernet or HDMI: those belong to the codec core.

## What is still open

1. **259 connections are open** (`build/open_report.txt`, net by net; `build/usb_report.txt` for the USB links).
   With two signal layers the router has less room than on the mainboard: 33 of the 104 USB nets are complete end to
   end, and the two upstream links from the ZERO 3E connectors (J50, J51) to the hubs are NOT among them. Those, and the
   SuperSpeed lines of bays 1-4 (68 connections), are the first things to finish by hand.
2. **The PoE primary side is only partly routed** (42 connections open). The transformer is a real part
   (Bourns PLN060-ER20A05S, planar, 8.0 mm, bottom face) and the low-voltage side is routed; on two layers the script
   cannot finish the 57 V side between its tightly packed parts.
3. What is routed is script output, not reviewed: pairs are coupled where a trunk could be found, nothing is
   length-tuned, and KiCad's rule check on the routed copper reports 39 items {'clearance': 3, 'track_dangling': 36}
   (`build/routing_report.txt`): loose track ends are untidy, the clearance items are defects to fix.
4. The battery module itself does not exist yet; see section 5.2b of the mainboard's `DESIGN_NOTES.md`.
5. BOM without manufacturer part numbers; the Gerbers in `fabrication/` are marked QUOTE-ONLY.
